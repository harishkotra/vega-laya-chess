A local decision model plays chess against a chat model. It does not generate text and it does not search: it picks from the moves it is offered, with a calibrated probability on each, and you can watch every choice.

Calibration

Top probability against measured accuracy, on the move question. A perfect engine lies on the diagonal.

Abstention

The engine refuses when it is not confident enough at the configured risk. If that flag is worth anything, accuracy when it abstains is lower than accuracy when it answers.

Regret, in moves

Zero means it found the fastest mate. One means it found a mate that takes one move longer. Two or more means no mate inside two moves.

Local decision models only

There is no cloud path and no chat model in this app. It runs decision models that are on this machine, loaded from a directory, and nothing about a decision leaves the machine. Downloading is consent gated per model: nothing is fetched until you tick the box and press the button for that model.

What gets downloaded, and what does not

Already have a checkpoint?

If a copy is already on this machine, in this app's folder or in the Hugging Face cache, it is used as is and nothing is downloaded. Point at another copy with --model-path vega=/path or --model-path laya=/path.

Why the 4B is skipped

The Vega checkpoint repository also holds a 4B model. Its backbone needs roughly 15 GB in float32 and this app never loads it, so the download allow-list excludes it. On a 16 GB machine the 0.8B is the one that fits.
